Dr. Brian Clarke

Dr. Brian D. Clarke was born and raised in Evansville, Indiana. He obtained his undergraduate degree in Chemistry at Indiana University in Bloomington in 1981 where he was inducted into Phi Beta Kappa as a junior and presented the Senior Award for the highest achievement. He attended Indiana University School of Medicine in Indianapolis and was inducted into Alpha Omega Alpha Medical Honor Society as a junior and graduated in 1985 with highest distinction. He completed his training at IU Medical Center in internal medicine residency in 1988 and in gastroenterology fellowship in 1990.

He is board certified in internal medicine and gastroenterology. He has been in private practice in gastroenterology with Gastroenterology Associates at Community Hospitals in Indianapolis until 1994. He founded Gastroenterology Consultants in 1994 and has established offices at Community North Professional Building and Carmel Surgery and Endoscopy Center.

His practice's philosophy is formed by his Christian faith to show genuine compassion to every patient and provide them with the best gastroenterological care possible.

His interests and expertise in gastroenterology are quite broad. He is very skilled in routine endoscopy and can perform advanced procedures such as therapeutic ERCP, sphincter of Oddi manometry, percutaneous endoscopic jejunoscopy tubes, Barrett’s esophagus ablation and EMR (endoscopic mucosal resection). He pioneered bariatric GI endoscopy, providing training to other bariatric endoscopists. He offers videocapsule endoscopy through his office in Carmel. He is skilled in the diagnosis and treatment of inflammatory bowel disease and provides in-office Remicade infusion therapy. He also is skilled in hepatology and liver biopsy for evaluation and treatment of acute and chronic liver diseases including hepatitis C.

He is a member of:

  • American Gastroenterological Association
  • American Society for Gastrointestinal Endoscopy
  • American College of Gastroenterology
  • American College of Physicians
  • Crohn's & Colitis Foundation of America
  • Indiana State Medical Association
  • Indianapolis Medical Society
