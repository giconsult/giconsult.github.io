Procedures We Perform

Dr Clarke and his friendly and experienced medical staff will walk you through each step of your procedure. Dr Clarke has helped pioneer many of the procedures used by physicians in the GI industry. Dr Clarke's experience along with a knowledgeable and friendly medical staff will make any patient's procedure as comfortable as possible.

  • Upper Endoscopy
  • Colonoscopy
  • Therapeutic ERCP
  • Bariatric GI endoscopy
  • Diagnosis and treatment of inflammatory bowel disease (IBS)
  • Hepatology
  • Evaluation and treatment of acute and chronic liver diseases

    If you have been scheduled for a procedure, and need procedure preparation information, please contact our office and a staff member will get you the information you need. Additionally, if you are a new patient, please be sure to fill out the New Patient Information Form and return it to our office.

    Procedure Preparation Information

    If you have a procedure scheduled and you have not received your preparation instructions, you can read, save or print the instructions relavant to your procedure listed below. Be sure you only use the preparation instructions our office instructed you to use. DO NOT use multiple preparations for your procedure. Please call our office if you have any questions.
  • Colonoscopy - CLENPIQ
  • Colonoscopy - Gallon
  • Colonoscopy - HalfLytely
  • Colonoscopy - Miralax Gatorade
  • Colonoscopy - MoviPrep
  • Colonoscopy - PLENVU
  • Colonoscopy - SUFLAVE
  • Colonoscopy - SUPREP
  • Colonoscopy - SUTAB
